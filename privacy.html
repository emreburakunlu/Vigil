Vigil Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Vigil is a recovery, sleep and strain tracker for iPhone. Your health data stays on your device. This policy explains what Vigil reads, where it goes, and what it does not do.

What Vigil reads

With your permission, Vigil reads the following from Apple Health: sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), workouts, running routes, activity, and body weight. Vigil uses this data only to calculate your Recovery, Sleep and Strain scores and to show your workouts and trends. Vigil does not write data to Apple Health.

What Vigil stores

Workouts you log in Vigil (movements, sets, reps and weight) and the app's calculated results are stored locally on your iPhone, and in the app's shared container so the Home Screen widgets can display them. This data is not sent to iCloud or to any server.

What Vigil does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No servers: Vigil does not transmit your data anywhere.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Vigil schedules them locally on your device. No notification data leaves your phone.

Your control

You can change or revoke Vigil's access to Apple Health at any time in Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Vigil. Deleting the app removes all data Vigil stored on your device.

Children

Vigil is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any personal information.
